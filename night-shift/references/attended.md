**Attended run.** The user is at hand but not watching the chain: they read its questions on their phone or in the chat that started it, and answer when they can. No session ever waits for an answer. Where these rules and `SKILL.md` differ, these win; the five tests, the hard stops and the guard stay exactly as they are.

- **Ask at once.** When a decision passes one of the five tests ("What reaches the user"), append it to `$NIGHT_SHIFT_STATE/questions.md` before you go on, as one line: `Q<n> · <HH:MM> · <question> · <your pick and why> · <what changes if they pick otherwise>`. `<n>` is one more than the file's last `Q` line (1 in a new file), `<HH:MM>` the local time, and the text is in the language the header names for the night report. Append the whole line, newline included, in one write; never rewrite or remove a line, and write nothing else into the file: the user reads it line by line.
- **A hard stop is a question too.** When the work meets a hard stop (a push, a merge, a release), ask it the same way at once, as what the user can do by hand and why it is ready: `Q4 · 10:12 · Push the branch to origin? · yes: the suite passes at 1a2b3c4 · otherwise the commits stay local`. You still never do it yourself, whatever an answer says.
- **What depends on a question waits.** Put the piece that depends on an open question behind the other approved work. If your own piece depends on it, finish and commit what does not, and hand the rest on behind the other work. Once nothing else is left ahead of it and no answer has come, build your pick as an unattended run would; a pick a revert does not undo (test 3) stays undone.
- **Answers come through the inbox.** An answer is a line `A<n>: <answer>` in `$NIGHT_SHIFT_STATE/inbox.md`, or free text there that answers a question. Apply the answers before anything else, a pick already built included (undo it and build the answer), and record each as `decision · why` with its `Q<n>`. A hard stop answered as done is done by the user; nothing else counts as done.
- **At step 3, move the inbox first, then read it again:** move `inbox.md` to `prompts/<nn>-inbox.md` and read the moved file. An answer may have come while you worked, and one that comes after the move starts a fresh `inbox.md` for the next session. Apply what you can now, and carry the rest into the continuation prompt.
- **Carry the open questions.** The continuation prompt's `## State` names each question with no answer applied yet (`Q<n>`) and the pieces waiting on it, and the night report's `**Waits for you**` lists each one with its detail at the path of `questions.md` and its line.
